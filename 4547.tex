\begin{Text}
Instructors will choose one of the following recommended texts:

\emph{Elementary Analysis}, 2\textsuperscript{nd} edition, by Kenneth A.
Ross, published by Springer, ISBN: 9781461462712 (eBook), Available free
through OSU libraries. Chapters 1--3.

\emph{Introduction to Real Analysis}, 4th edition, by Bartle \&
Sherbert, published by Wiley, ISBN: 9780471433316. Chapters 1.1--5.4.
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item
  Sequences and their limits.
\item
  Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem and Cauchy's criterion. Lim sup's and lim
  inf's.
\item
  Convergence and absolute convergence of series. Tests for convergence.
\item
  Continuity and limits of functions. Uniform continuity.
\end{TopicsList}

\begin{SuggestedSchedule}
\week{1}{The real numbers; completeness axiom; suprema and infima.}
\week{2}{Sequences and their limits; limit theorems.}
\week{3}{Monotone sequences; subsequences.}
\week{4}{Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem.}
\week{5}{Cauchy's criterion; Cauchy sequences.}
\week{6}{Lim sup and lim inf. \emph{Midterm I.}}
\week{7}{Series; convergence and absolute convergence.}
\week{8}{Comparison, ratio, and root tests.}
\week{9}{Alternating series; rearrangements.}
\week{10}{Limits of functions.}
\week{11}{Continuity; algebra of continuous functions. \emph{Midterm II.}}
\week{12}{Continuous functions on closed bounded intervals; intermediate value theorem.}
\week{13}{Uniform continuity.}
\week{14}{Monotone and inverse functions; review.}
\finalsweek{Final examination.}
\end{SuggestedSchedule}
