\begin{Purpose}
The acquaintance with rigorous probability theory, its history and its multiple connections, will better prepare honor students for graduate studies and will help them get involved in research at earlier stages of their careers.
\end{Purpose}

\begin{Text}
Vary, for example: • \emph{Heads and Tails. An Introduction to Limit Theorems in Probability}, E. Lesigne, AMS, ISBN 0821837141 • \emph{Elementary Probability Theory with Stochastic Processes}, Kai Lai Chung • Elementary Probability Theory, Kai Lai Chung and Farid AitSahlia,, 4\textsuperscript{th} Edition, Springer, 2003
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item Historical origins of probability.
\item Diverse ways of sampling, allocation, models.
\item Random variables, expectation, moments.
\item Important distributions.
\item Limit theorems: law of large numbers, central limit theorem.
\item Random walks and Markov chains.
\item Statistical independence in analysis and number theory.
\end{TopicsList}
