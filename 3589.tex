\begin{Purpose}
This course is designed as an introduction to the concepts encountered in financial mathematics for students who don’t have a background in continuous-time stochastic calculus.
\end{Purpose}

\begin{Text}
\emph{Stochastic Calculus for Finance I, The Binomial Asset Pricing Model}, by Shreve, published by Springer, ISBN: 9780387249681
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item First principles; assumptions about stock behavior and description of basic financial instruments; put and call options.
\item Arbitrage, and no-arbitrage pricing.
\item One-period and multi-period models; replication and hedging.
\item Conditional expectations.
\item Martingales and Markov processes.
\item Change of measure.
\item Utility functions and the capital asset pricing model.
\item Stopping times and American derivatives.
\item Random walks and passage times.
\end{TopicsList}
