\begin{Text}
\emph{Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos}, Steven H. Strogatz, published by
Westview Press, ISBN 9780738204536
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item
  One-dimensional flows: geometric way of thinking; fixed points and
  stability; population growth and other applications.
\item
  Bifurcations in one-dimensional flows: saddle-node, transcritical and
  pitchfork bifurcations; imperfect bifurcations.
\item
  Theory: existence; uniqueness; continuous dependence.
\item
  Phase planes: phase portraits; vector fields; nullclines; fixed
  points; stability;
\item
  Linear systems: classification of linear systems; what do linear
  systems say about the nonlinear system?
\item
  Limit cycles; introduction; Poincare-Bendixson theorem; conservative
  systems.
\item
  Bifurcations of two-dimensional flows; Hopf bifurcation theorem.
\item
  XPPAUT: phase planes; bifurcations; applications.
\item
  Global bifurcations: homoclinic orbits; Poincare map; stability of
  periodic orbits.
\item
  Singular perturbations: Relaxation oscillators; averaging.
\item
  One-dimensional maps: Logistic map.
\item
  Smale horseshoe: symbolic dynamics.
\item
  Applications.
\end{TopicsList}
