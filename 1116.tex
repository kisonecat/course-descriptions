\begin{Purpose}
The emphasis in this course is on intuitive understanding and developing some facility for applying mathematical ideas to problem solving.
\end{Purpose}

\begin{Text}
\emph{Excursions in Modern Mathematics}, 8\textsuperscript{th} edition, by Tannenbaum, Pearson, ISBN 9780321825735
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item Graph theory: graphs, Euler and Hamilton circuits, algorithms for Traveling Salesman Problem, spanning trees, etc.
\item Voting \& apportionment: preference ballots; apportionment paradoxes; Congressional apportionment; methods of Jefferson, Adams, and Webster.
\item Patterns \& growth: Fibonacci and recursive sequences, golden ratio, population growth models: linear, exponential, and logistic.
\item Symmetry: Rigid motions, rosettes, friezes, rudiments of group theory.
\item Counting \& probability: counting principles, permutations and combinations, multiplication rule, randomness, probability.
\item Fractals: recursive definitions, standard examples (Koch snowflake, Sierpinski gasket etc.), self-similarity, fractional dimension.
\item Linear programming: mixture problems, examples in low dimension, corner point principle, algorithms.
\end{TopicsList}
