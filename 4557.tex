\begin{Text}
\emph{Partial Differential Equations, an Introduction}, 2\textsuperscript{nd} edition,
Walter A.Strauss, published by Wiley, ISBN: 0471548685.
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item
  Definition of a PDE, linearity; solution of first-order linear
  (transport) equation; modeling with PDEs.
\item
  Well-posed problems, initial- and boundary conditions; secondorder
  equations; classification into types; the wave equation.
\item
  Causality and energy; diffusion equation; diffusion on the whole line.
\item
  Solution of the wave and diffusion equations on a half-line; diffusion
  and waves with sources.
\item
  Separation of variables for the wave equation, Dirichlet, Neumann and
  Robin conditions.
\item
  Fourier series; sine and cosine series; orthogonality and general
  Fourier series; completeness and convergence.
\item
  Midterm. Gibbs phenomenon.
\item
  Laplace's equation; maximum principle; rectangular coordinates.
\item
  Poisson's formula; Laplace's equation in circular coordinates.
\item
  Green's identities; maximum principle; Dirichlet principle; Green's
  second identity.
\item
  Green's functions; symmetry; half-space and sphere.
\item
  Wave equation in two and three dimensions; energy; causality;
  Huyghens' principle.
\item
  Rays and characteristics; relativistic geometry; sources; the
  diffusion equation.
\item
  The Schrödinger equation; the hydrogen atom.
\end{TopicsList}
