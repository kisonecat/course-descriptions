\begin{Purpose}
To offer an experience in problem solving in mathematics for interested and talented students beyond what they would encounter in a standard program. It is preparation for the National Putnam Mathematics Exam. This course is repeatable to a maximum of 6 credit hours, and is graded S/U. This course may not be counted in a major or minor program in Mathematics.
\end{Purpose}

\begin{TopicsOutline}
Interesting special problems as chosen by the instructor.
\end{TopicsOutline}
