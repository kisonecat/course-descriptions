## Catalog Description

Continuation of Math 4547.

## Prerequisite

C- or better in 4547, or credit for 548.

## Text Instructors will choose one of the following
recommended texts:

*Elementary Analysis*, 2^nd^ edition, by Kenneth A. Ross,
published by Springer, ISBN: 978-1-4614-6271-2 (eBook), Available free
through OSU libraries. Section 19 and Chapters 4-6.

*Introduction to Real Analysis*, 4^th^ edition, by Bartle
& Sherbert, published by Wiley,\
ISBN: 9780471433316, Section 5.4 and Chapters 6-10.

## Topics List

1.  Uniform continuity (Recap from 4547).

2.  Derivatives, Mean Value Theorem, L'Hospital's rule.

3.  Sequences and series of function, Pointwise and uniform convergence,
    Power series.

4.  Riemann integral.

5.  Exponential and logarithmic functions.

## Suggested Schedule

- **Week 1.** Recap of uniform continuity.
- **Week 2.** The derivative; algebra of derivatives; chain rule.
- **Week 3.** Mean Value Theorem and consequences.
- **Week 4.** L'Hospital's rule; Taylor's theorem.
- **Week 5.** Sequences of functions; pointwise convergence.
- **Week 6.** Uniform convergence and its consequences. *Midterm I.*
- **Week 7.** Series of functions; Weierstrass M-test.
- **Week 8.** Power series; radius of convergence.
- **Week 9.** Differentiation and integration of power series.
- **Week 10.** The Riemann integral: definition and basic properties.
- **Week 11.** Integrability criteria; classes of integrable functions. *Midterm II.*
- **Week 12.** Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.
- **Week 13.** Interchange of limits and integrals; improper integrals.
- **Week 14.** Exponential and logarithmic functions; review.
- **Finals week.** Final examination.
