\begin{Text}
Vary, for example:  An Introduction to Complex Function Theory, by B.P. Palka  Elementary Theory of Analytic Functions of One or Several Complex Variables, by H. Cartan  Complex Analysis, 2nd edition, by Bak-Newman  Complex Analysis with Applications, by Silverman
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item Complex numbers, Riemann's sphere. Complex functions, elementary functions, Möbius transformations.
\item Holomorphic functions, Cauchy-Riemann equations.
\item Line integrals. Cauchy’s integration theorem and its consequences.
\item Harmonic functions.
\item Sequences and series of holomorphic functions. Power series, analytic functions.
\item Isolated singularities, meromorphic functions, the calculus of residues.
\item Conformal mappings, the Riemann mapping theorem.
\item Geometric principles.
\item Mittag-Leffler's and Weierstrass's expansions of meromorphic functions.
\item Analytic continuation, Riemann surfaces.
\item Applications to number theory, geometry, physics.
\end{TopicsList}
