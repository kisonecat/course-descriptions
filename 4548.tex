\begin{Text}
Instructors will choose one of the following recommended texts:

\emph{Elementary Analysis}, 2\textsuperscript{nd} edition, by Kenneth A.
Ross, published by Springer, ISBN: 9781461462712 (eBook), Available
free through OSU libraries. Section 19 and Chapters 4--6.

\emph{Introduction to Real Analysis}, 4\textsuperscript{th} edition, by
Bartle \& Sherbert, published by Wiley,\\
ISBN: 9780471433316, Section 5.4 and Chapters 6--10.
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item
  Uniform continuity (Recap from 4547).
\item
  Derivatives, Mean Value Theorem, L'Hospital's rule.
\item
  Sequences and series of function, Pointwise and uniform convergence,
  Power series.
\item
  Riemann integral.
\item
  Exponential and logarithmic functions.
\end{TopicsList}

\begin{SuggestedSchedule}
\week{1}{Recap of uniform continuity.}
\week{2}{The derivative; algebra of derivatives; chain rule.}
\week{3}{Mean Value Theorem and consequences.}
\week{4}{L'Hospital's rule; Taylor's theorem.}
\week{5}{Sequences of functions; pointwise convergence.}
\week{6}{Uniform convergence and its consequences. \emph{Midterm I.}}
\week{7}{Series of functions; Weierstrass M-test.}
\week{8}{Power series; radius of convergence.}
\week{9}{Differentiation and integration of power series.}
\week{10}{The Riemann integral: definition and basic properties.}
\week{11}{Integrability criteria; classes of integrable functions. \emph{Midterm II.}}
\week{12}{Fundamental Theorem of Calculus.}
\week{13}{Interchange of limits and integrals; improper integrals.}
\week{14}{Exponential and logarithmic functions; review.}
\finalsweek{Final examination.}
\end{SuggestedSchedule}
