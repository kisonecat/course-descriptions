## Catalog Description

This course will serve as an introduction to algebraic topology, with a
view toward persistent homology of point clouds for applications to data
analysis. Homology of simplicial complexes over a field with a focus on
building up intuition about homology moving to a specialized notion of
persistent homology of persistence modules. Real-world applications to
data analysis will be provided.

## Prerequisites

C- or better in 2568 and 3345.

2.  Metric Topology

5.  Persistent Homology

6.  Persistence Diagrams

7.  Structures on the Space of Barcodes

8.  Applications

## Suggested Schedule

- **Week 1.** Review of linear algebra: vector spaces, rank, quotients.
- **Week 2.** Metric spaces and metric topology.
- **Week 3.** Simplicial complexes; chains and boundary maps.
- **Week 4.** Homology of simplicial complexes over a field.
- **Week 5.** Computing homology; examples and intuition.
- **Week 6.** Simplicial maps and induced maps on homology.
- **Week 7.** Point clouds; Vietoris-Rips and Cech complexes. *Midterm.*
- **Week 8.** The nerve theorem; stability of associated spaces.
- **Week 9.** Filtrations and persistence modules.
- **Week 10.** Persistent homology; the structure theorem.
- **Week 11.** Persistence diagrams and barcodes.
- **Week 12.** Bottleneck and Wasserstein distances; the stability theorem.
- **Week 13.** Structures on the space of barcodes; vectorizations.
- **Week 14.** Applications to data analysis; project presentations.
- **Finals week.** Final examination or final projects.
