## Catalog Description

4547-4548 involves advanced calculus covering: sequences, limits,
continuity, differentiation, Riemann integral, sequences and series of
functions, Taylor series, and improper integrals.

## Prerequisite

C- or better in 3345; or credit for 345.

## Texts

Instructors will choose one of the following
recommended texts:

*Elementary Analysis*, 2^nd^ edition, by Kenneth A. Ross,
published by Springer, ISBN: 9781461462712 (eBook), Available free
through OSU libraries. Chapters 1-3.

*Introduction to Real Analysis*, 4th edition, by Bartle &
Sherbert, published by Wiley, ISBN: 9780471433316. Chapters 1.1-5.4.

## Topics List

1.  Sequences and their limits.

2.  Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem and Cauchy\'s criterion. Lim sup's and
    lim inf's.

3.  Convergence and absolute convergence of series. Tests for
    convergence.

4.  Continuity and limits of functions. Uniform continuity.

## Suggested Schedule

- **Week 1.** The real numbers; completeness axiom; suprema and infima.
- **Week 2.** Sequences and their limits; limit theorems.
- **Week 3.** Monotone sequences; subsequences.
- **Week 4.** Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem.
- **Week 5.** Cauchy's criterion; Cauchy sequences.
- **Week 6.** Lim sup and lim inf. *Midterm I.*
- **Week 7.** Series; convergence and absolute convergence.
- **Week 8.** Comparison, ratio, and root tests.
- **Week 9.** Alternating series; rearrangements.
- **Week 10.** Limits of functions.
- **Week 11.** Continuity; algebra of continuous functions. *Midterm II.*
- **Week 12.** Continuous functions on closed bounded intervals; intermediate value theorem.
- **Week 13.** Uniform continuity.
- **Week 14.** Monotone and inverse functions; review.
- **Finals week.** Final examination.
