## Catalog Description

Vector operations; Jacobian and change of variables; div, grad and curl;
Green\'s Stokes\', and divergence theorems; applications.

## Prerequisite

C- or better in 2153, 2162.xx, 2173, or 2182H; or credit for 254.xx,
263.xx or 263.01H.

### Exclusions

Not open to students with credit for Math 4182H, 264H, 513 or 551.

## Text

*Advanced Calculus*, 5^th^ edition, by Kaplan, published
by Pearson, ISBN: 9780201799378

## Topics List

1.  Vector operations, multiple integrals, line and surface integrals.

2.  Vector operators: div, grad, and curl.

3.  Jacobians and change of variables.

4.  Green\'s Theorem, Stokes theorem.

5.  Divergence Theorem.

6.  Applications.

## Suggested Schedule

- **Week 1.** Vector algebra; dot and cross products; lines and planes.
- **Week 2.** Vector-valued functions; curves, arc length, curvature.
- **Week 3.** Partial derivatives; the chain rule; the gradient.
- **Week 4.** Double integrals; iterated integration.
- **Week 5.** Triple integrals; cylindrical and spherical coordinates.
- **Week 6.** Jacobians and change of variables. *Midterm I.*
- **Week 7.** Line integrals; work; conservative fields and potentials.
- **Week 8.** Surfaces and surface integrals; flux.
- **Week 9.** Divergence, gradient, and curl; vector identities.
- **Week 10.** Green's Theorem.
- **Week 11.** Stokes' Theorem. *Midterm II.*
- **Week 12.** The Divergence Theorem.
- **Week 13.** Applications: fluid flow, gravitation, electromagnetism.
- **Week 14.** Applications: potential theory; curvilinear coordinates; review.
- **Finals week.** Final examination.
