\begin{Purpose}
The purpose of the course is to prepare prospective middle school math teachers. In particular, it intends to deepen and extend prospective teachers’ connections among topics in mathematics through the study of the history of mathematics, as well as continuing to develop their ability to reason with and communicate that knowledge.
\end{Purpose}

\begin{Text}
Math through the Ages: A Gentle History for Teachers \& Others, Expanded Edition, by Berlinghoff \& Bouvea, published by Mathematical Association, ISBN: 9780883857366

Course Packet
\end{Text}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item History of Mathematics, from ancient to modern times.
\item Development of number systems, operations, geometry, trigonometry, algebra, calculus, statistics, and probability.
\item Applications: modeling real-world topics.
\item Problem solving (a theme throughout the course).
\end{TopicsList}
