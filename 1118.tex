\begin{FollowupCourses}
There are really no follow-up courses. To start any other mathematics sequence will probably involve beginning at an appropriate entry level course. Students interested in further course work in mathematics should consult the Mathematics Advisors in 250 Mathematics Bldg.
\end{FollowupCourses}

\begin{Purpose}
The majority of the audience is made up of Architecture majors for whom the course is a requirement. The intent of the course is to introduce these students to the mathematics inherent in 2D and 3D design. Moreover, there is an emphasis on similar figures and the issues that arise when scaling lengths, areas, and volumes.
\end{Purpose}

\begin{TopicsList}
\item Geometric models, transformations, matrices.
\item Plane tessellations, concepts of symmetry.
\item Polyhedra: Platonic and Archimedean.
\item Invariants: area, volume, Euler characteristic.
\item Mathematics of perspective drawing.
\item String art: curves defined as envelopes of tangent lines, ruled surfaces.
\item Discrete curvature: Descartes theorem and beyond.
\item Higher dimensions: tesseracts and other 4-D polyhedra.
\end{TopicsList}
